# LeadLens — B2B Lead Cost Calculator

**LeadLens** is a free, privacy-friendly B2B sales planning tool by **[Galaxy Connect](https://www.galaxyconnect.in/)**. It helps sales teams understand what purchased business leads and marketing enquiries actually cost.

**[Try the live calculator on Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/spaces/galaxyconnectindia/b2b-lead-calculator)**

## Features

- Cost per acquired lead = total spend / leads acquired
- Cost per usable lead = total spend / usable leads
- Cost per qualified prospect = total spend / qualified prospects
- Usable lead rate = usable leads / acquired leads
- Qualified / acquired rate = qualified prospects / acquired leads
- Supports INR, USD, EUR and GBP; instant updates in your browser

### Example

For INR 5,000 total spend, 500 acquired leads, 375 usable leads and 45 qualified prospects, the costs are INR 10.00 per acquired lead, approximately INR 13.33 per usable lead, and approximately INR 111.11 per qualified prospect. Usability rate is 75% and qualified/acquired rate is 9%.

## Run locally

Download or clone this repository and open `index.html` in a browser. No dependencies or sign-in required.

## GitHub Pages

Go to **Settings > Pages**, select **Deploy from a branch**, then **main** and **/(root)**. The page will be available on the organization's GitHub Pages domain after deployment. The working [Hugging Face demo](https://huggingface.co/spaces/galaxyconnectindia/b2b-lead-calculator) remains available independently.

## Privacy and limitations

Calculations run locally in the browser. This app does not upload your entered figures. Results are planning metrics, not a verification of lead quality, accuracy, consent, or suitability for outreach. It does not predict sales or guarantee business outcomes.
